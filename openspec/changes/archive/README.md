# Archived OpenSpec changes

Directories here are historical records. They are non-authoritative, are excluded
from the active change set, and must not be synchronized into capability specs.
Current product and architecture authority is defined by the instructor assignment,
`docs/requirements.md`, accepted ADRs, `docs/architecture.md`, and active specs under
`openspec/specs/`.

An archived change may contain deferred, rejected, superseded, or incomplete ideas.
Its task checkboxes and delta specs describe history, not current MVP requirements.
